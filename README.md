# Fast & Furious

**Fast & Furious** is an action film series centered on illegal street racing, heists, and family.  
It is known for its thrilling car chases, stunts, and the famous line — *“I don’t have friends, I got family.”*

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Main Characters
- **Dominic Toretto** – played by Vin Diesel  
- **Brian O’Conner** – played by Paul Walker  
- **Letty Ortiz** – played by Michelle Rodriguez  
- **Roman Pearce** – played by Tyrese Gibson  

## 🚘 Highlights of the Series
1. **Fast Five (2011)** – Introduced the heist theme and The Rock as Hobbs.  
2. **Furious 7 (2015)** – Emotional farewell to Paul Walker.  
3. **Fast X (2023)** – Continues the saga with even bigger action sequences.  

## 🔧 Why I Like It
I enjoy *Fast & Furious* because of the racing and car theme of the movies.
